Before you start.

Can synthesis always keep up at 10×?

The 0.5 to 10× range controls playback of prepared audio. Uncached synthesis throughput depends on the model and hardware. Initial downloads and model loading also take time.

Can this be the default EPUB app?

The Windows installer can register the reader in Open with and Default apps. Choose the default in Windows Settings; the installer does not replace UserChoice.

Does it render every EPUB exactly?

The current native renderer supports a subset of EPUB HTML/CSS. Complex layouts and scripts have limitations. The project does not claim full EPUB conformance.

Are the voices and models also MIT?

MIT applies to original project code. External models, voices, dictionary data, and runtimes have their own terms. Neural model weights are not included in the app downloads.
