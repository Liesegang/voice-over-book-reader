The two small audio fixtures contain an original 220 Hz sine wave and no speech.
They may be used, modified, and redistributed under CC0-1.0.

Generated with FFmpeg for decoder regression tests; FFmpeg is not required to
build, run, or test the reader. `chapters.m4b` uses AAC-LC with two MP4 chapters,
and `tone.mp3` uses MPEG layer III. Each recording is two seconds at 24 kHz.
