Kokoro and the native Japanese frontend
======================================

Kokoro 82M v1.0 model and ONNX conversion
https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M
https://huggingface.co/onnx-community/Kokoro-82M-v1.0-ONNX
Apache License 2.0 (complete text in Misaki-LICENSE.txt).
The unmodified model cards, including their training-data acknowledgements,
and exact source revisions are retained in this directory and SOURCES.json.
Locally generated *.duration-v1.onnx files modify the ONNX graph outputs to
expose the existing duration prediction. They do not retrain the model.

audio.cpp v0.9.3 native Japanese phoneme rules
Copyright 2026 ShugoAI LLC
https://github.com/0xShug0/audio.cpp/tree/v0.9.3
Apache License 2.0; complete original notice in audio.cpp-LICENSE.txt.
Voice Over Book Reader adapts these rules to Rust with source-range tracking,
its own normalization, punctuation handling, and speech timing integration.

Misaki 0.9.4 G2P rules and resources
https://pypi.org/project/misaki/0.9.4/
https://github.com/hexgrad/misaki
Apache License 2.0; complete original text in Misaki-LICENSE.txt.
Misaki's cutlet.py states that it was adapted from the MIT-licensed Cutlet:
Copyright (c) 2020 Paul O'Leary McCann
https://github.com/polm/cutlet
The complete original MIT notice is retained in Cutlet-LICENSE.txt.
No independent Python Cutlet or Misaki package is installed by the reader.

Optional NVIDIA runtimes retain their own proprietary terms separately:
NVIDIA-CUDA-cuBLAS-LICENSE.txt and NVIDIA-cuDNN-LICENSE.txt are unchanged
copies from the exact pinned PyPI wheels. The latter contains an older SDK
supplement; it is not replaced by a newer online agreement. The original
cuDNN 9.8 acknowledgements page is retained as supplemental attribution in
NVIDIA-cuDNN-ACKNOWLEDGEMENTS.html. These texts do not indicate that NVIDIA
binaries are bundled with this reader, or that they are covered by MIT.

UniDic and MeCab are separate from these licenses. The verified Kokoro GGUF
retains the UniDic package's MIT notice and the dictionary's AUTHORS, COPYING,
BSD, GPL and LGPL texts. The BSD option supplied by the UniDic Consortium is
used for dictionary data. Pinned fugashi wheels supply native MeCab and retain
LICENSE.mecab and LICENSE.fugashi. The reader does not load Python code.

The reader's own MIT license does not relicense these components. Keep their
original notices, model cards, and applicable terms with separate copies.
